Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 17, 2026  |  Last Updated: July 17, 2026

Who We Are

Bayside Dysphagia Diagnostics, PLLC ("BDD," "we," "us," or "our") is a mobile speech-language pathology practice providing FEES (Fiberoptic Endoscopic Evaluation of Swallowing) evaluations to healthcare facilities on Cape Cod and the South Shore of Massachusetts. Our website is located at www.baysidefees.com.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information submitted through this website.

Information We Collect

We collect information only when you voluntarily submit it through our contact form. This may include:

  • Your name and job title
  • Your facility name
  • Your email address and phone number
  • Any message or inquiry you choose to include

Important: Our contact form is not HIPAA-compliant and is intended for facility representatives only. Please do not submit any patient names, dates of birth, medical record numbers, diagnoses, or other protected health information (PHI) through this form.

We do not collect information through cookies, tracking pixels, or behavioral analytics tools. We do not use Google Analytics or any third-party advertising technology.

How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through the contact form is used solely to respond to your inquiry and communicate with you about our services. We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your contact information with any third party for marketing purposes.

How We Store and Protect Your Information

Contact form submissions are transmitted to us via EmailJS, a third-party email delivery service. EmailJS processes your form submission and delivers it to our inbox. EmailJS's privacy policy is available at emailjs.com/legal/privacy-policy.

Our website is served over HTTPS (TLS encryption) to protect data in transit. We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect any information we receive, consistent with applicable Massachusetts law, including 201 CMR 17.00 (Standards for the Protection of Personal Information of Residents of the Commonwealth).

We retain contact inquiries only as long as necessary to respond to and fulfill the purpose of your request. We do not maintain a database of website contacts for ongoing marketing.

Third-Party Services

This website uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Fonts — We load typefaces from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com). Google may receive your IP address when fonts are loaded. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • EmailJS — Used to transmit contact form submissions. See EmailJS's Privacy Policy.

We do not use advertising networks, social media tracking pixels, or behavioral analytics tools.

Clinical Privacy (HIPAA)

Bayside Dysphagia Diagnostics, PLLC provides clinical services to healthcare facilities. In that capacity, we handle protected health information (PHI) in accordance with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA). We execute Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) with our client facilities as required.

This Privacy Policy governs only our website. It does not describe how PHI is handled in the course of providing clinical services, which is governed separately by our HIPAA policies and the BAA with each client facility.

Your Rights

You may request to review, correct, or delete any personal information you have submitted to us through this website. To make such a request, contact us using the information below. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

This website is directed to healthcare facility professionals and is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the website after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
