Meet Your Dysphagia Specialist

Melanie Reynolds, MS, CCC-SLP

Founder · SLP · Dysphagia Specialist

Melanie Reynolds

MS, CCC-SLP  ·  Founder & Dysphagia Specialist

Melanie Reynolds is a certified Speech-Language Pathologist specializing in dysphagia management for medically complex patients, with more than 25 years of clinical experience across Cape Cod Hospital, South Shore Hospital, Boston Medical Center, and Spaulding Rehabilitation Hospital.

She has more than 20 years of experience performing FEES and Modified Barium Swallow Studies, and has taught FEES training courses and guest lectured at Boston University Sargent College.
