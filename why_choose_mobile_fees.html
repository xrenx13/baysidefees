What Is FEES?

Why Bayside Dysphagia Diagnostics?

FEES (Fiberoptic Endoscopic Evaluation of Swallowing) is a cost-effective and convenient tool for assessing swallowing disorders that provides direct visualization of the swallow in the patient's natural eating environment. A thin, flexible endoscope is passed through the nose for a real-time view of the pharynx and larynx while the resident eats and drinks actual food and liquid textures — not a barium-coated substitute. Results are available immediately, and the procedure is generally well-tolerated.

  • No transport — done in the resident's room, usually within 1–3 days of referral
  • No radiation — safe to repeat as often as clinically needed
  • Real diet — evaluates the food and liquids the resident actually eats
  • Founder-led care — every study performed by a 25+ year dysphagia specialist, not a rotating contractor pool
  • FEES-specific expertise — an instructor of FEES training courses with 20+ years of performing FEES in various medical settings
  • True partnership — providing patient, family and staff education as well as direct collaboration with your facility SLP on care planning
  • Local & responsive — dedicated coverage across Cape Cod and the South Shore, with fast scheduling
